# Master Prompt: Blender-Style Game Thumbnail

Back to [[Roblox Dev Resources]]

Attach gameplay screenshots (and the game logo, if you have one) when using this prompt. Fill in the scene description line before sending.

```
Create a professional 16:9 video game thumbnail that looks genuinely handmade in Blender by an experienced 3D artist, using the attached gameplay screenshots as the primary visual references.

Gameplay Accuracy
- Study the attached screenshots carefully.
- Match the game's actual character designs, proportions, costumes, materials, environments, and art style.
- Preserve the game's original graphical quality, whether low-poly, cartoon, stylized, or realistic.
- Do not invent new characters, accessories, structures, or unnecessary details.
- The thumbnail must clearly resemble the actual game.

Handmade Blender Style
- Authentic Blender Eevee/Cycles rendering appearance.
- Clean 3D geometry, smooth shading, and accurate materials.
- Professional lighting with a soft key light, subtle fill light, and gentle rim lighting.
- Natural ambient occlusion, realistic shadows, and balanced contrast.
- Slight depth of field to create depth while keeping characters sharp.
- Bright, attractive colors without excessive saturation.
- Avoid overly glossy plastic textures, exaggerated bloom, artificial outlines, and the typical AI-generated illustration look.

Composition
- Show an exciting, natural gameplay moment with approximately 2–4 main characters or subjects.
- Use dynamic but believable poses that match the game's actual animations and mechanics.
- Create a clear focal point with professional camera framing.
- Keep the background faithful to the supplied gameplay screenshots.
- Make the scene readable at small thumbnail sizes.
- Maintain a consistent camera, lighting, and rendering style across multiple thumbnails.

Character & Environment Quality
- Correct anatomy with no extra, missing, duplicated, or fused limbs.
- No strange bumps, ears, warped faces, or malformed accessories.
- No floating objects, clipping, broken geometry, or impossible poses.
- Keep costumes and equipment properly attached.
- Avoid random particles, excessive motion effects, or unnecessary visual clutter.
- Prioritize simple, clean, believable 3D models over exaggerated AI details.

Logo & Branding
If a game logo is attached, place it in the upper-left corner while preserving its exact design, lettering, colors, and proportions. Do not redesign it or add additional text.

Final Requirements
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Resolution: Ideally 1920 × 1080
- Professional, handmade Blender render appearance.
- Consistent lighting, materials, camera quality, and color grading.
- Faithful to the actual gameplay screenshots.
- No visible AI generation mistakes.

SCENE DESCRIPTION:
i attached screenshots of my game

IMPORTANT: The final result should look like a manually posed, professionally lit Blender render using the game's real models and environments — not an AI-generated recreation of a different game.
```
