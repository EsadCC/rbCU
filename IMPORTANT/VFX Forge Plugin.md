## How it fits the pipeline

```
Roblox Studio
↓
VFX Forge 1.4.5 (plugin)
↓
VFX Forge → Emit Module
↓
ReplicatedStorage.EmitModule   (place-specific, generated by the plugin)
↓
shared.vfx   (runtime interface)
```

- `EmitModule` is **place-specific**. Never assume another place already has it.
- The source of the generated module is the plugin's `lib/emit` ModuleScript.
- Do not manually recreate, rewrite, optimize, or rename `EmitModule`.
