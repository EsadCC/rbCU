## Transitions
Biomes should blend believably — highlands fade to snow, desert borders canyon, etc. No hard seams.

## Half-scale adaptation
At 5 km, each biome is a fraction of original size. Prioritize the HIGH ones for Phase 2 (first high-quality region = Coast/Beach starting zone).
