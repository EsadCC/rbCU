# Map Overview

*Status: DRAFT — geography from model knowledge, needs verification against real references.*

## What Ragnarok is
A free official ARK map, Viking-themed, combining elements of The Island and Scorched Earth. Notable for its size, varied biomes, and Viking longships/runestones.

## Our version
Half-scale recreation (~5 km × 5 km working target). See [[Map Scale]] and [[Coordinate System]].

## Layout concept
A roughly square landmass surrounded by ocean, with:
- Temperate/viking coastline (SW) — starting zone
- Green highlands (NW/W)
- Frozen north (N)
- Redwood forest (center)
- Volcano (center)
- Desert with dunes + giant skeleton (E/SE)
- Canyon/gorge with bridges (center-S)
- Swamps + jungle (S)
- Ocean islands + the Maw (offshore)
