## Working assumption (provisional)
For blockout purposes only:
- Assume original Ragnarok playable area ≈ **10 km × 10 km** (100 km²).
- Half-scale → **5 km × 5 km** target.
- In Roblox studs (1 km ≈ 3570 studs):
  - **~17,850 studs × 17,850 studs.**

This sits safely inside the ±32,000 terrain limit.

## Object scale
- **Do NOT halve individual objects.**
- Players, creatures, trees, buildings, props stay full Roblox scale.
- Only the *map layout and travel distances* are reduced.
